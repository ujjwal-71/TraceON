Privacy Policy & Ephemeral Data Lifecycle

Effective Date: September 2026 • TRACEON Defensive Cyber Platform

1. Core Zero-Retention Principle

TRACEON is architected strictly as an ephemeral diagnostic tool. When you upload or synchronize an email:

  • RAM-Only Processing: Email bodies, headers, and attachments are processed strictly in volatile computer memory (RAM).
  • Zero Permanent Database: We do not store, index, or archive raw emails on persistent disks or external cloud databases.
  • 10-Minute Ephemeral TTL: Memory records automatically expire and are permanently wiped by an automated background sweep after 10 minutes.
  • Instant Manual Purge: You may instantly destroy all in-memory traces of an analyzed case by clicking the "Purge Evidence from RAM" button on the scorecard.

2. Zero Third-Party Tracking & Cookies

TRACEON operates with Zero Third-Party Advertising Cookies and zero tracking scripts (no Google Analytics, no Facebook Pixels). We use strictly browser-local localStorage to store non-identifying UI preferences (such as dark mode and your preferred local extension API endpoint).

3. Cryptographic Hashes for Court Evidence

To support digital forensics under Section 65B of the Indian Evidence Act and Section 63 of Bharatiya Sakshya Adhiniyam (BSA) 2023, TRACEON generates SHA-256 evidence checksums of analyzed artifacts. These mathematical hashes verify artifact integrity without storing the underlying message.

4. Data Sharing & Third Parties

We do not sell, rent, or monetize any diagnostic telemetry. When live geolocation resolution is enabled, public IP addresses (and no email content) are queried against standard autonomous system routing tables to triangulate server geography.
